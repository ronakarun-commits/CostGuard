## What the numbers say

**Detectors.** The simple causal baseline (same-weekday median, robust z-score on log cost,
materiality filter) wins: ~98% precision, ~80% recall, detection within a day. It is also the
only one usable for live alerting, because it never looks at future data. STL looks good on paper
but, being fitted on the whole series, it smears level shifts into the trend and flags ordinary
days around them (low precision, late detection). Isolation Forest has the best recall (it catches
level shifts the baseline adapts to) but roughly half its incidents are false alarms. Ensembling
baseline + iforest is the obvious next experiment.

Misses are mostly small anomalies in small series (e.g. a 3x spike on a $5/day dev service),
filtered out by the $10/day materiality rule. That is a deliberate trade: an alert nobody acts on
is worse than no alert.

**Risk model.** Raw ETS bounds are breached 9–17% of days instead of 5%, and Kupiec rejects them
on every seed. Breaches cluster inside injected anomaly windows: the model captures
business-as-usual noise but not regime changes (incidents, forgotten resources). Rather than guess
a heavier-tailed distribution, the bounds are **conformally calibrated** on the model's own past
errors, out-of-sample. Exceedances fall to 2–9%; calibration can no longer be rejected on 3 of 5
seeds; month-end 90% interval coverage rises from 67–85% to 81–96%; point accuracy is unchanged
(month-end MAPE 0.4–2.1%, forecast made on day 7/14/21).

Remaining gap: when a regime change happens *after* the calibration window (seed 123), bounds
are still too narrow for a while. Shorter calibration windows adapt faster but are noisier.

## Limitations
- Synthetic data: anomaly shapes and noise are modelled, not observed. Real bills have credits,
  refunds, tax and month-start charges (e.g. support plans) that this generator does not include.
- Daily granularity: Savings Plans are consumed hourly; intra-day variation is ignored.
- Event-level scoring with a ±3-day tolerance; different tolerances change the numbers.
